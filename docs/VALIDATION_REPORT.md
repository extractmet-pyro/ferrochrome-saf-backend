# FeCr-TWIN — Validation report

*Extractmet Private Limited · FeCr-TWIN v1.0 · report date 2026-10-29*

> **Status: SYNTHETIC / NOT PLANT-VALIDATED.** Every result below compares the twin with itself, with a
> synthetic "virtual plant" generated by the twin (with deliberate hidden mismatch), or with published
> indicative ranges. **No plant data were used. Nothing in this report is evidence of accuracy on a real
> furnace.** Section 8 lists what a site validation must add.

## 2. Closure (V1–V3)

Mass and energy are carried as conserved states (element-resolved species inventories; total enthalpy on a
formation-enthalpy basis), and temperature is obtained by inverting enthalpy. Closure is therefore exact to
floating-point precision by construction, and an independent ledger re-computes it from stream totals each
step. Measured on this release:

| Run | Worst element residual (% of input) | Energy residual (MW) |
|---|---|---|
| CI gate, 6 h nominal | 4.57 × 10⁻¹³ | −1.7 × 10⁻¹⁴ |
| Scenarios A–L (each 12–24 h) | < 10⁻¹⁰ (reported 0.0 kg/h Cr) | ≤ 3.6 × 10⁻¹² |

Exact closure proves bookkeeping consistency, **not** that the physics is right.

## 3. Nominal case against indicative ranges (V4)

Synthetic 45 MVA closed AC furnace "Industry-X", charge per t ore: 163 kg metallurgical coke, 40 kg coal,
85 kg quartzite; ore blend 55 % lumpy / 45 % pellet.

| Quantity | Twin (scenario A, 24 h) | Indicative published range |
|---|---|---|
| SEC, kWh/t HC FeCr (tapped basis) | 3 306 | ~3 100–4 200, depending on pre-treatment |
| Cr recovery to metal, % | 90.7 | ~75–92 |
| Slag Cr2O3-equivalent, wt % | 9.1 | ~5–15 |
| Metal Cr / C / Si, wt % | 64.0 / 6.97 / 3.4–3.5 | 50–70 / 6–8.5 / 0.5–5 |
| Metal tap temperature, °C | ~1 640 | ~1 550–1 700 |
| Off-gas CO (dry), % | ~82–83 | ~75–90 |
| Off-gas H2 (dry), % | ~6 | a few % (volatiles, moisture) |
| Production, t/h | ~10.9 (261 t/24 h) | consistent with ~45 MVA class |

Long-run stability: an 84 h nominal run from warm start keeps coke-bed fill 0.69 → 0.83, metal Si 3.3–3.5 %,
SEC 3.29 MWh/t, Cr recovery 90.5 %, electrode tip depth 3.5 m. (An earlier calibration at 172 kg coke/t ore
accumulated carbon after volatile cracking was added — fill 1.56 and Si 4.5 % after 84 h — and was corrected.)

## 5. Static vs dynamic (V6)

The charge-mix planner's steady-state balance at the nominal recipe gives SEC 3 368 kWh/t, metal Si 3.16 %
and carbon supply/demand 0.96; the 24 h dynamic run gives 3 306 kWh/t and 3.4–3.5 % Si. Differences come from
the static model's surrogate recoveries (calibrated to the twin, not to a plant) and from inventory change
inside the 24 h window.

## 6. State estimator (V7)

Nine-state EKF with out-of-sequence fusion of delayed lab assays, 24 h nominal run, synthetic gateway with
noise, 62 tags. Error = estimate − hidden twin truth, evaluated after 4 h (1 200 samples).

| State | Bias | RMSE | Share of errors inside 90 % interval |
|---|---|---|---|
| Metal Cr, wt % | −0.13 | 0.18 | 1.00 |
| Metal C, wt % | −0.02 | 0.04 | 1.00 |
| Metal Si, wt % | −0.09 | 0.09 | 1.00 |
| Metal inventory, t | −0.12 | 0.20 | 1.00 |
| Slag inventory, t | +1.08 | 1.18 | 1.00 |
| Coke-bed fill, – | 0.000 | 0.003 | 1.00 |

Caveat: the estimator's process model is the same twin that generates the truth (matched model). Coverage of
1.00 means the intervals are **conservative** (too wide), not that the estimator is exact. On a plant, model
mismatch will increase errors; process noise must be tuned from data.

## 7. Hybrid ML and MPC (V8, V9)

**Campaign.** 16 days, 180 taps, generated by the twin with a hidden mismatch the physics model does not know:
fines-dependent chromite mass transfer, a different slag current-path fraction and coke reactivity drift.
Chronological split 60/20/20 (train 4–216 h, val 218–288 h, test 290–360 h), no shuffling; lag-1 lab features.

| Target | Physics-only test RMSE | Hybrid test RMSE (active model) | Hybrid R² | 90 % interval coverage on test |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| SEC, kWh/t | 104.0 | 66.5 (ridge) | 0.23 | 0.97 |
| Cr recovery, % pts | 5.83 | 0.89 (ridge) | 0.97 | 0.75 |
| Slag Cr2O3-eq, wt % | 3.72 | 0.33 (ridge) | 0.99 | 0.81 |

The gradient-boosting candidates stay in *shadow* because ridge validated better. Test-set PSI shows drift on
most features (the campaign walks through recipes), so the intervals under-cover for Cr recovery and slag
Cr2O3: this is reported, not hidden. Good metrics here demonstrate the pipeline on a known synthetic mismatch,
**not** real-world accuracy (lag-1 features also benefit from autocorrelation).

**MPC comparison, scenario D (high-ash reductant), 24 h, recommendations applied in simulation every 3 h:**

| Policy | Metal t | SEC kWh/t | Cr recovery % | Mean Si % | Constraint-violation steps | Mean compute s |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Baseline operator rule | 258.6 | 3 345 | 90.81 | 3.54 | 0 | – |
| Physics MPC | 258.0 | 3 334 | 90.50 | 3.48 | 0 | 2.0 |
| Hybrid MPC | 258.0 | 3 334 | 90.51 | 3.48 | 0 | 2.1 |

Reading: MPC lowered SEC by ~0.3 % and Si by 0.07 pts at the cost of ~0.3 pts Cr recovery and 0.6 t metal. The
benefit is small and mixed in this synthetic case; objective weights decide the trade-off and must be set with
the plant. The hybrid corrections change little here because the MPC moves are small. Neither result is a
claim about plant savings.

## 8. What a site validation must add

1. ≥ 4-week reconciled assay campaign (ores, reductants, fluxes, metal, slag, dust) and ≥ 30 taps for Cr,
   Si, C, SEC and tap-temperature validation with agreed tolerances.
2. Electrical model fit (resistance, current, depth, slips) and gas-analyser lag/accuracy.
3. Slag liquidus/viscosity against lab data or a licensed database for the site's slag window.
4. Kinetic and heat-transfer parameter fit with identifiability report and uncertainty bands.
5. Chronological ML re-training on plant data; drift monitoring in shadow mode for ≥ 3 months.
6. MPC benefit study in shadow mode (recommendations logged, not applied) against actual operator moves.
